## 1. Creative Philosophy & Visual Principles

1. **Photograph Why the Chemical Exists**: Never make the plastic container the sole story. Show the physical cleaning challenge: heavy oil, road film, manure acids, carbon crusts, aluminium oxidation, and heated washdown physics.
2. **Industrial, Scientific, Corporate**: Avoid domestic car shampoo aesthetics, excessive "snow foam" mountains, generic laboratory clipboards, or medical/pharmaceutical tropes.
3. **Restrained Colour Palette**: Ground scenes in dark graphite, cast iron, 304 stainless steel, and warm industrial white, with Alkota signature orange accents.
4. **Label Integrity**: Never generate fake chemical labels, fabricated hazard pictograms, or invented product codes.
